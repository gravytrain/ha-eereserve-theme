# EEReserve

EEReserve is the Home Assistant theme for the Double E Reserve farm dashboards
and their companion custom cards.

It provides standard Home Assistant colors alongside a documented `home-ui-*`
theme contract that compatible cards can consume without hardcoding a palette.

## Included palettes

- **Meter Register:** graphite surfaces, brass accents, and equipment colors.
- **Farmhouse Ledger:** olive and parchment treatments in light and dark modes.
- **Agribuddy:** crop events, planner states, alerts, and health indicators.

The theme can also be used without the companion cards as a complete light/dark
Home Assistant theme.
